ROBO SCALPER PRO

Automated Trading System

About the Project

ROBO SCALPER PRO is a Python-based automated trading project designed to integrate with the cTrader Open API.

The system focuses on real-time Forex market analysis, short-term scalping strategies, price movements, buying and selling pressure, and automated trading execution.

Technology and Risk Management

The project incorporates market data analysis, technical indicators, position sizing, stop-loss, take-profit and exposure controls.

Development and Testing

The system is under development and is being tested in a demo trading environment before any potential live deployment.
